Presented under the *Pedagogical Innovations & Creative AI* track together with the paper based on practice-based research and autoethnography [*"From Numbness to Narrative: A Filmmaker’s AI-Augmented Journey to Emotional Resonance in Storytelling, Unveiling the Potential of a Structured Vocabulary of Emotions. A Case-Study in Rapid Emotional Decoding for Enhanced Creative Communication."*](https://georgiamihalcea.github.io/publication/2024-11-11-iceri-paper)

This presentation outlined the foundational framework for emotional semantic modeling, demonstrating how structured metadata vocabularies can be deployed within the audiovisual industry to bypass creative barriers and optimize content discoverability.
