Presented under the *Pedagogical Innovations & Creative AI* track. This presentation outlined the foundational framework for emotional semantic modeling, demonstrating how structured metadata vocabularies can be deployed within the audiovisual industry to bypass creative barriers and optimize content discoverability. Peer-reviewed paper and poster presentation introducing an autoethnographic framework for rapid emotional decoding and metadata-driven content architecture in the age of digital overproduction.
